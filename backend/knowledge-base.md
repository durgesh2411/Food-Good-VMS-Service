# Food Good VMS - Knowledge Base

## Platform Overview
Food Good VMS is a comprehensive Volunteer Management System focused on food donation and community service. Our platform connects volunteers with meaningful opportunities to make a positive impact in their communities.

## Core Features

### 1. User Registration & Authentication
- **How to register**: Go to the registration page, fill in your details including name, email, phone number, and upload an avatar image
- **Login process**: Use your registered email and password to access your account
- **Account types**: Regular users can become volunteers, while admin accounts have additional management capabilities

### 2. Volunteer System
- **Becoming a volunteer**: Click the "Become a Volunteer" button on the homepage after logging in
- **Volunteer benefits**: Access to exclusive events, ability to create posts, participate in voting systems
- **Volunteer responsibilities**: Participate in food donation drives, community events, and help organize activities

### 3. Donation System
- **How to donate**: Navigate to the "Donate Us" page and choose your donation amount
- **Payment methods**: We use Razorpay for secure payment processing
- **Donation tracking**: All donations are tracked and you'll receive confirmation
- **Impact**: Your donations directly support food distribution and community programs

### 4. Event Management
- **Finding events**: Check the Events page for upcoming community activities
- **Event participation**: Register for events you're interested in
- **Creating events**: Volunteers and admins can create new community events
- **Event types**: Food drives, community gatherings, awareness campaigns, skill workshops

### 5. Posts & Community
- **Creating posts**: Volunteers can share updates, photos, and stories from their activities
- **Post approval**: Admin approval ensures quality content
- **Community engagement**: Like, comment, and share posts from other volunteers

### 6. Star Voting System
- **Purpose**: Rate and review volunteer activities and events
- **How to vote**: Access the "Vote Stars" section to rate completed activities
- **Impact**: Helps improve future events and recognizes outstanding volunteers

### 7. Announcements
- **Stay updated**: Check announcements for important platform news
- **Admin announcements**: Official updates about new features, policies, or events
- **Notification system**: Get notified about relevant announcements

### 8. Dashboard
- **Personal overview**: View your volunteer statistics, recent activities, and achievements
- **Activity tracking**: Monitor your contribution hours and impact metrics
- **Profile management**: Update your information and preferences

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Registration
**Q: I forgot my password, how can I reset it?**
A: Currently, password reset functionality is being implemented. Please contact support for assistance.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Contact support to update your email address with proper verification.

**Q: Why do I need to upload an avatar?**
A: Profile pictures help build community trust and make interactions more personal.

### Volunteering
**Q: What are the requirements to become a volunteer?**
A: Simply register on the platform and click "Become a Volunteer." No special qualifications required, just enthusiasm to help!

**Q: How many hours should I volunteer?**
A: There's no minimum requirement. Volunteer based on your availability and passion.

**Q: Can I volunteer for specific causes only?**
A: Yes, you can choose events and activities that align with your interests.

### Donations
**Q: Is my donation secure?**
A: Yes, we use Razorpay, a secure and trusted payment gateway used by millions.

**Q: Can I get a donation receipt?**
A: Yes, you'll receive confirmation after successful donation processing.

**Q: What's the minimum donation amount?**
A: Any amount is welcome - every contribution makes a difference!

### Events
**Q: How do I know about new events?**
A: Check the Events page regularly and watch for announcements.

**Q: Can I suggest event ideas?**
A: Yes! Use the feedback system or contact administrators with your ideas.

**Q: What if I can't attend an event I registered for?**
A: Please inform the organizers as soon as possible through the platform.

### Technical Issues
**Q: The website is loading slowly**
A: Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. Contact support if issues persist.

**Q: I can't upload my avatar image**
A: Ensure your image is under 5MB and in JPG, PNG, or GIF format.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Use the AI Support chat feature or send feedback through the platform.
