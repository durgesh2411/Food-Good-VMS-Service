# Lift for Upliftment NEET Prep NGO - Knowledge Base

## Platform Overview
Lift for Upliftment is a comprehensive NGO platform focused on NEET preparation support and educational upliftment. Our platform connects volunteers with meaningful opportunities to help students prepare for NEET examinations and achieve their medical career dreams.

## Core Features

### 1. User Registration & Authentication
- **How to register**: Go to the registration page, fill in your details including name, email, phone number, and upload an avatar image
- **Login process**: Use your registered email and password to access your account
- **Account types**: Regular users can become volunteers, while admin accounts have additional management capabilities

### 2. Volunteer Management
- **Becoming a volunteer**: Click "Become a Volunteer" after registration to help with NEET preparation support
- **Volunteer work submission**: Submit your educational support activities with photos and descriptions
- **Work approval**: Admin reviews and approves volunteer work submissions
- **Hours tracking**: System automatically tracks your volunteer hours spent helping NEET aspirants

### 3. Events System
- **Event creation**: Admins can create NEET prep events, study sessions, mock tests, and educational workshops
- **Event registration**: Users can register for upcoming educational events and study sessions
- **Event status**: Events can be active, completed, or cancelled
- **Event participation**: Track attendance and participation in NEET preparation activities

### 4. Posts & Announcements
- **Creating posts**: Share your NEET preparation experiences, study tips, and success stories
- **Post approval**: Admin moderation ensures quality educational content
- **Announcements**: Important platform updates and NEET preparation news
- **Content visibility**: Posts are visible to all users once approved

### 5. Donation System
- **Making donations**: Secure payment processing through Razorpay to support NEET aspirants
- **Donation tracking**: View your donation history
- **Impact visibility**: See how donations are being used to support NEET preparation

### 6. Star Voting System
- **Vote for volunteers**: Recognize outstanding volunteers who help with NEET preparation
- **Voting criteria**: Based on contribution to student success and dedication
- **Leaderboard**: See top-rated educational volunteers
- **Recognition system**: Gamification to encourage participation in educational support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### About Lift for Upliftment NGO

**Q: When and where was Lift for Upliftment founded?**
A: Lift for Upliftment was founded in 2015 in Pune, Maharashtra. Our main office is located in Camp area, opposite SBI Treasury Branch, Pune.

**Q: What is the success rate of your NEET preparation program?**
A: Our overall NEET qualification rate is 26.9%, which is significantly higher than the national average of 15.7%. Over 3,456 students have qualified NEET through our programs since 2015.

**Q: How many students have you helped get into medical colleges?**
A: As of 2024, 2,134 of our students have successfully secured admissions in medical colleges, with 1,456 getting into government medical colleges and 678 into private institutions.

**Q: What makes your NEET preparation different from coaching institutes?**
A: Unlike commercial coaching centers, we focus on underprivileged students, provide completely free education, offer personalized mentoring, and have a volunteer-driven model with medical professionals as tutors.

**Q: Is Lift for Upliftment a registered NGO?**
A: Yes, we are registered under the Society Registration Act, 1860, have 80G certification for tax-exempt donations, and are in the process of obtaining FCRA registration for international funding.

### Student Eligibility & Admission

**Q: Who can join Lift for Upliftment NEET preparation program?**
A: Students from economically weaker sections (family income below ₹3 lakhs annually) who have completed 10+2 with PCB subjects and scored minimum 60% in 12th standard are eligible.

**Q: Is there any fee for NEET preparation?**
A: No, all our services are completely free including study materials, mock tests, counseling sessions, and even meals during classes.

**Q: How do you select students for the program?**
A: Selection is based on financial need assessment, academic merit (minimum 60% in 12th), entrance test performance, and personal interview focusing on determination and commitment.

**Q: What documents are required for admission?**
A: Income certificate, 10th & 12th mark sheets, caste certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card, passport-size photographs, and bank account details for scholarship disbursement.

**Q: Can students from rural areas apply?**
A: Yes, 68% of our students come from rural backgrounds. We provide hostel facilities and transportation support for deserving rural students.

### Program Structure & Curriculum

**Q: What is the duration of the NEET preparation program?**
A: We offer both 1-year and 2-year programs. The 2-year program starts after 11th standard, while the 1-year intensive program is for 12th pass students.

**Q: What study materials do you provide?**
A: Comprehensive study packages including NCERT books, reference materials, practice question banks with 15,000+ questions, previous year papers (2010-2024), and access to our digital learning platform.

**Q: How many mock tests are conducted?**
A: We conduct 150+ mock tests throughout the year, including 50 full-length tests, 60 subject-wise tests, and 40 chapter-wise assessments, all designed as per latest NEET pattern.

**Q: Do you provide online classes?**
A: Yes, we offer hybrid learning with both offline and online classes. Our digital platform has 2,400+ video lectures accessible 24/7 through our mobile app.

**Q: What is the class schedule?**
A: Regular batch: 6 hours daily (9 AM - 3 PM), 6 days a week. Weekend batch: 8 hours on Saturday and Sunday for working students or those in regular college.

### Volunteer Opportunities

**Q: How can I become a volunteer tutor?**
A: Medical graduates, engineering graduates, and subject matter experts can apply. Requirements include subject expertise, teaching aptitude test, background verification, and commitment of minimum 4 hours weekly.

**Q: What qualifications do volunteer tutors need?**
A: Minimum graduation in PCB subjects, preference for medical graduates/students, teaching experience preferred but not mandatory, and passion for social service.

**Q: Do volunteers get any compensation?**
A: Volunteers serve without monetary compensation but receive certificates, recognition awards, networking opportunities with medical professionals, and skill development training.

**Q: How many volunteer tutors do you have?**
A: Currently, we have 890 active volunteer tutors including 267 medical students and doctors, 124 subject matter experts, and others from various educational backgrounds.

**Q: What support do you provide to volunteers?**
A: Teaching methodology training, digital tools access, transportation reimbursement for rural center visits, annual appreciation events, and career guidance for volunteer students.

### Donations & Funding

**Q: How can individuals donate to Lift for Upliftment?**
A: Donations can be made through our website, bank transfer, cheque, or UPI. We accept one-time donations, monthly contributions, and sponsorship for specific students.

**Q: Are donations tax-exempt?**
A: Yes, donations are eligible for 50% tax deduction under section 80G of the Income Tax Act. We provide proper receipts for all donations.

**Q: What is the minimum donation amount?**
A: There's no minimum limit. Even ₹100 can provide study materials for one student for a week. We believe every contribution matters in a student's journey.

**Q: How is donation money utilized?**
A: 88% goes directly to programs (study materials, faculty, infrastructure), 12% for administration. Complete financial transparency reports are published annually.

**Q: Can corporates contribute through CSR?**
A: Yes, we are eligible for CSR funding under education category. We have received ₹7.8 crores through CSR partnerships with 45+ companies since 2015.

### Technology & Resources

**Q: Do you have a mobile app for students?**
A: Yes, "Lift Learning" app provides access to video lectures, practice tests, doubt resolution, study planner, and progress tracking. Available on both Android and iOS.

**Q: How do students get their doubts resolved?**
A: Multiple channels: in-person doubt sessions, WhatsApp groups with tutors, video calling with experts, and AI-powered doubt bot for instant basic clarifications.

**Q: What digital resources are available?**
A: 2,400+ video lectures, 15,000+ practice questions, interactive 3D models for biology, chemistry simulation labs, physics concept animations, and personalized learning paths.

**Q: Do you provide counseling services?**
A: Yes, we have qualified counselors for academic guidance, career counseling, stress management, and mental health support. Many students face anxiety during NEET preparation.

### Success Stories & Results

**Q: Can you share some notable success stories?**
A: Priya from a farmer family in Satara scored 650 in NEET 2023 and is now in AIIMS. Rohit, son of a daily wage worker, got into KEM Hospital. We have 100+ such inspiring stories.

**Q: What is the average score improvement of your students?**
A: Students typically improve by 180 marks from their initial mock test scores. Physics shows 82% improvement, Chemistry 78%, and Biology 85%.

**Q: How many of your students get government medical college seats?**
A: 68% of our successful candidates secure government medical college seats, which is remarkable considering the high competition and our focus on economically weaker students.

**Q: Do you track students after NEET qualification?**
A: Yes, we maintain alumni network and provide continued support during medical college admissions, education loans, and even career guidance during their medical studies.
